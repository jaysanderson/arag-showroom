# Showcase script — ARAG Showroom

Runtime: approximately 2 minutes 40 seconds. Recorded by `showcase/record.spec.ts` (`make showcase`)
against a locally booted portal with a bootstrap administrator and the committed content snapshot.
No credentials, no network calls beyond the Mermaid CDN.

Audience: Progress, and ISV partners in the Progress Agentic RAG partner network.

---

## 0:00 — The programme, in one screen

> "This is the Agentic RAG partner-accelerator programme. Three open-source reference products
> Progress ISV partners can white-label, extend, or build on — and take to market through their
> existing reach."

*Shot: `01-home-hero.png`. The hero, with the four counted numbers beside it: products, documented
endpoints, automated tests, and how many run on the Node standard library alone.*

> "Every number on this page is counted out of the repositories during the content sync. Nothing
> here is estimated."

## 0:20 — One foundation under all three

*Shot: `02-platform.png`. The platform section, with the architecture diagram rendered.*

> "Underneath all three sits one platform: a typed ARAG client, an HTTP toolkit that enforces the
> API contract, a deterministic mock Knowledge Box so any of them runs offline with no account, and
> a shared UI kit and repo template. A fourth accelerator starts where the third finished."

## 0:40 — What a partner gets

*Shots: `03-for-partners.png`, `06b-engagement-models.png`, `06c-white-label.png`.*

> "For a partner: a working product on day one, Apache-2.0, with rebranding by configuration rather
> than by forking, documented extension points, and four ways in depending on whether you resell,
> embed, extend or build. All of that lives on its own page, so the product pages stay pointed at a
> partner's own customers."

## 1:00 — The three accelerators

*Shots: `04-products.png`, `05-market.png`, `06-traction.png`, `07-roadmap.png`.*

> "Document Processing turns documents into validated records. Call Analysis turns recorded calls
> into labelled, citable evidence. VoiceBridge keeps a grounded, cited brief in front of whoever is
> on a live conversation. Each one has its own page, its own roadmap, and its own measured proof."

## 1:20 — One accelerator, in full

*Shots: `08-product-hero.png`, `09-what-you-get.png`, `10-how-it-works.png`, `10b-proof.png`.*

> "A product page reads the way a partner's own customer would want it to: the benefit first, then
> what you get, then how it works with the real architecture diagram, then the proof — measured on
> the product's own repository — and the recorded walkthrough. It is a white-label asset: the whole
> page renders under whatever brand the deployment is configured with."

## 1:40 — Asking for access

*Shot: `11-request-partner-access.png`.*

> "The documentation, the enablement tracks and the live demos are invite-only. A partner asks here.
> Nothing is emailed anywhere and no third party is involved: the request lands in the portal."

## 1:55 — The administrator's side

*Shots: `12-sign-in.png`, `13-access-requests.png`, `14-invitation-created.png`, `15-users.png`,
`16-audit-log.png`.*

> "An administrator signs in, sees the request waiting, and issues a one-time invitation with a role
> attached — here, partner globally and operator on Call Analysis specifically. The link is shown
> once. Every sign-in and every administrative action is in the audit log."

## 2:20 — The partner's side

*Shots: `17-accept-invitation.png`, `18-portal.png`, `19-product-overview.png`, `20-docs-browser.png`,
`21-enablement.png`, `22-showcase.png`.*

> "The partner opens the link, chooses their own password, and lands in the portal. They get the
> positioning, the full documentation rendered as web pages with the architecture diagrams drawn,
> the enablement tracks — worked solutions included, because they are a partner — and the showcase
> recording. What they cannot see is not linked: the role decides."

## 2:35 — API-first, like everything else

*Shot: `23-api-reference.png`.*

> "And the portal is API-first like the accelerators it presents: every capability is on a versioned,
> documented `/api/v1`, validated against the same OpenAPI document the contract tests use."
